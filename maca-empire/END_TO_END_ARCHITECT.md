# 🏛️ MaCA Empire: End-to-End Architectural Blueprint (Phase 1)
**Project Status:** Production Ready | **Total Active Agents:** 15 | **Competition ID:** P-0038

This document provides a comprehensive breakdown of the 15 specialized AI agents currently deployed and pushed to the repository. These agents form the foundation of the institutional-grade legal and financial orchestrator.

---

## 🏗️ 1. The Core Infrastructure (Foundation)
These are the primary agents handling the daily essential compliance for and individuals.

1.  **A1 / Supreme Tax:** Integrated intelligence for Income Tax, GST, and TDS. It specializes in predictive tax optimization and live penalty monitoring.
2.  **A2 / Banking & Credit:** Focuses on RBI compliance and dispute resolution for banking transactions. It includes tools for credit score restoration and nodal officer drafting.
3.  **A3 / Notice & Disputes:** The "AI Judge" module. It drafts high-stakes notice replies and simulates legal risks to predict court outcomes.
4.  **A4 / Payroll & HR:** Automates Indian salary compliance (PF, ESI, TDS) and provides advisory on ESOP structures and labor law.

---

## 📈 2. The Growth Block (B2B & Velocity)
These agents are designed for startups and fast-moving corporate entities.

5.  **A5 / Corporate Counsel:** The digital ROC officer. Handles startup legalities, incorporation, and IP/Trademark protection strategies.
6.  **A6 / Voice CA:** Our flagship multimodal interface. Allows conversational legal advisory in Hindi and English, bridging the accessibility gap.
7.  **A7 / Deal Reviewer:** High-stakes contract analysis. Performs deep-link reviews for SPA (Share Purchase Agreements) and SHA (Shareholding Agreements).
8.  **A8 / Filing Ops:** Automation for e-court filings and professional RTI (Right to Information) drafting.

## 💎 3. The Elite Block (Specialized Investigation)
High-authority agents for corporate forensic and cross-border operations.

9.  **A12 / Forensic Audit:** An investigative engine designed to detect corporate fraud, financial leakages, and anomalies in balance sheets.
10. **A13 / Trade & Forex:** Specialist in FEMA compliance, EXIM (Export-Import) logistics, and cross-border currency regulations.

## 👑 4. The Empire Block (Crown Jewels)
The most sophisticated agents in the ecosystem, handling the future of tech and wealth.

11. **A22 / CryptoTax Pro:** Specialized VDA (Virtual Digital Assets) intelligence. Tracks the 30% tax rule and 1% TDS with real-time tax meters.
12. **A23 / ESG Compass:** Built for SEBI BRSR compliance. Tracks carbon credits, GHG emissions, and corporate sustainability reporting.
13. **A24 / HeirGuard:** Digital succession planning. Drafts digital wills and manages asset transmission across generations.
14. **A25 / Data & AI Safety:** Focuses on the DPDP Act 2023 (Digital Personal Data Protection) and the EU AI Act governance framework.
15. **A26 / The Oracle:** The terminal agent. It synthesizes 50 years of market wisdom with live global feeds for strategic foresight.
