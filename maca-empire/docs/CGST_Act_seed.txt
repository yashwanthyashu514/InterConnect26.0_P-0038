
Section 47 of the CGST Act 2017: (1) Any registered person who fails to furnish the returns required under section 39 or section 44 or section 45 or section 52 by the due date shall be liable to pay a late fee of one hundred rupees for every day during which such failure continues. 
Note: For GSTR-1, the current late fee is typically ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST) for the delay.
