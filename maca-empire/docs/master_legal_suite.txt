[A3 COMPLIANCE - ROC/MCA DEADLINES]
- FORM AOC-4: Financial statements of company filing. Due: 30 days from AGM date. Penalty: ₹100/day.
- FORM MGT-7: Annual return of company. Due: 60 days from AGM date. Penalty: ₹100/day.
- ADT-1: Appointment of auditor. Due: 15 days from AGM.

[A4 PAYROLL - TDS/PF/ESI RULES]
- PF CONTRIBUTION: Employer + Employee (12% each). Due: 15th of following month.
- ESI CONTRIBUTION: Employer (3.25%) + Employee (0.75%). Due: 15th of following month.
- TDS ON SALARY (192): Mandatory if income > basic exemption. Deduct monthly using Slab rates.

[A8 COURT FILER - ESCALATION]
- GRIEVANCE FIRST: Complaint to nodal officer.
- ESCALATION 30D: Ombudsman (RB-IOS 2026) if bank doesn't reply in 30 days.
- WRIT PETITION (Art. 226): High Court escalation if specialized tribunal/ombudsman fails due to bias or error in law.
