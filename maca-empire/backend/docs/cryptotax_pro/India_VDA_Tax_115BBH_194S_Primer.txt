India VDA Tax Primer: Section 115BBH and Section 194S

Background:
Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) include crypto assets and NFTs covered by the Income-tax Act framework.

Section 115BBH (Tax on VDA Income):
- Income from transfer of a VDA is taxed at a special rate.
- No deduction is allowed except cost of acquisition.
- Set-off of loss from transfer of VDA against any other income is generally not permitted.
- Carry-forward of such loss is also restricted under the special regime.

Section 194S (TDS on VDA Transfer):
- Tax deduction at source applies on payment/consideration for transfer of VDA beyond prescribed threshold.
- Deductor responsibilities differ for specified persons and non-specified persons.
- In barter or exchange transactions, compliance may require ensuring tax payment before release of consideration.

Compliance Records:
- Maintain transaction-level ledger with timestamp, wallet/exchange references, INR value, acquisition cost, transfer consideration, and fees.
- Reconcile exchange statements with internal books.
- Preserve working papers for valuation methodology.

Practical Classification:
- Investment vs trading classification impacts treatment in financial statements and reporting posture.
- Maintain consistency in accounting policy and documentation.

GST and FEMA Touchpoints:
- GST and cross-border remittance implications may arise depending on business model.
- Evaluate separately with legal-advisory support for exchange/platform operations.
