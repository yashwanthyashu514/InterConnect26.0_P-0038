Probate & Succession Checklist (India) — HeirGuard

High-level flow:
1) Identify estate assets: bank accounts, demat, mutual funds, real estate, insurance, PF/EPF, business interests.
2) Determine governing law and forum: religion-specific personal laws, Indian Succession Act, local jurisdiction of property/court.
3) Check if Will exists and whether probate is required in the relevant jurisdiction.

Core documents (typical):
- Death certificate
- Identity and address proofs of legal heirs
- Will (original) if available
- Property documents: sale deed, mutation records, encumbrance certificate
- Bank statements / passbooks, fixed deposit receipts
- Demat holdings statement, CAS statement for mutual funds
- Nomination details for EPF/insurance/demat where applicable

Court-facing (probate / letters of administration):
- Petition with schedule of assets
- Affidavits, witness statements, valuation documents
- Public notice / newspaper publication proof where required

Operational controls:
- Build asset register with evidence links.
- Track nomination vs legal heir conflicts.
- Maintain a timeline: notice period, objections, court hearings.

Risks:
- Missing original Will and witness issues
- Multiple claimants and disputes
- Cross-jurisdiction assets and compliance delays
