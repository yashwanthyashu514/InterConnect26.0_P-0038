SEBI BRSR Primer (India) — ESG Compass

What is BRSR:
Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) is SEBI’s ESG disclosure framework for listed entities. It standardizes sustainability reporting and aligns with India’s stewardship and stakeholder governance expectations.

Who must file:
Top listed entities (by market capitalization / SEBI applicability) must file BRSR as part of annual reporting. Requirements can expand by regulatory updates.

Core idea:
Disclose governance, strategy, risks, and performance on environmental, social, and governance parameters using defined metrics and principles.

BRSR Core:
SEBI introduced BRSR Core as a subset of key ESG parameters for assurance and comparability. It drives higher data quality and auditability.

Implementation steps:
1) Define ESG data owners across functions (HR, procurement, EHS, finance).
2) Build data collection templates and a control calendar.
3) Establish audit trails for each metric (source system, approvals).
4) Validate methodology (boundaries, emission factors, baselines).
5) Prepare for limited assurance on BRSR Core where applicable.

Common pitfalls:
- Inconsistent boundaries (subsidiaries/contractors excluded without rationale)
- Missing audit trails for ESG KPIs
- Narrative statements without metric support
- Not reconciling ESG metrics with financial disclosures and risk sections
