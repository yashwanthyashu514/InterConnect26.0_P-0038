DPDP Enterprise Compliance Checklist (Operational)

1) Data Inventory and Mapping:
- Identify personal data fields across web, app, CRM, support tools, and analytics.
- Map data flows: collection, storage, transfers, retention, and deletion.
- Mark high-risk processing activities and processors.

2) Consent and Notice Architecture:
- Publish layered privacy notice in plain language.
- Record consent timestamp, source, and policy version.
- Implement purpose limitation and consent withdrawal workflow.

3) Data Principal Rights Workflow:
- Build SLA-based request handling for access, correction, and erasure.
- Create authenticated request channel and appeal-ready audit logs.
- Maintain reasoned responses for rejected requests.

4) Processor and Vendor Controls:
- Execute data processing agreements with confidentiality and security clauses.
- Ensure sub-processor transparency and flow-down obligations.
- Conduct periodic vendor risk assessments.

5) Security Controls:
- Encryption in transit and at rest for sensitive systems.
- RBAC and least privilege for internal data access.
- Centralized logging and anomaly detection.
- Incident response runbook with breach triage.

6) Breach Management:
- Define materiality thresholds and legal escalation matrix.
- Prepare regulator and user notification templates.
- Run tabletop simulations quarterly.

7) Retention and Deletion:
- Define record retention schedule by purpose and legal basis.
- Implement automatic deletion or anonymization on expiry.
- Verify deletion in backups and downstream systems where feasible.

8) Governance:
- Appoint privacy lead and accountability owners by business unit.
- Maintain policy register and version control.
- Run periodic internal audits and staff training.

9) Child Data Safeguards:
- Implement age-gating where relevant.
- Enforce parental consent verification.
- Disable behavioral profiling and targeted ads for children.

10) Evidence Pack for Audit:
- Consent logs
- Rights request logs
- Incident logs
- Vendor assessments
- Policy/training records
