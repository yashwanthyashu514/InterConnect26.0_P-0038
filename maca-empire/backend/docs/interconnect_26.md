# InterConnect 26.0 — Official Rulebook

## 1. About the Event
InterConnect 26.0 is an Interdisciplinary Innovation Challenge organized by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, GMIT. Unlike a traditional hackathon, it follows an idea-driven collaboration model — participants can contribute either a problem idea or technical skills to build real solutions that improve campus life.
Inspired by Google Summer of Code & GirlScript Summer of Code.

## 2. Objectives
*   Encourage innovative thinking across all branches of study.
*   Promote interdisciplinary, heterogeneous team formation.
*   Transform raw ideas into practical solutions and functional prototypes.
*   Improve campus life through student-led technology.
*   Foster teamwork, technical skills, and problem-solving mindset.
*   Build a startup, research, and open-source contribution culture.

## 3. Who Can Participate
*   **Open To:** All students of GM University campus (interdisciplinary participants are higher priority).
*   **Team Size:** Individual participation allowed; teams are highly encouraged.
*   **Participation Mode:** Idea contributor OR technical skill contributor.

## 4. How It Works
1.  **Register:** Sign up on the event portal: [inter-connect-self.vercel.app](https://inter-connect-self.vercel.app)
2.  **Idea Submission:** Post a problem statement OR list your technical skills (AI, web, hardware, etc.).
3.  **Team Formation:** The portal lets contributors choose and join ideas based on their skills.
4.  **Build:** Teams develop their solution over the event window (1 Apr – 17 Apr 2026).
5.  **Mentorship:** Coordinators assign work and guide teams throughout the development period.
6.  **Final Demo:** Present your working prototype to the judging panel on 18 April 2026.

## 6. Judging Criteria
| Criteria | Weightage | What We Look For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% | Is the idea original and impactful? |
| Technical Implementation | 25% | Is the solution well-built and functional? |
| UI / UX & Design | 20% | Is it user-friendly and visually clear? |
| Impact & Feasibility | 20% | Can it realistically improve campus life? |
| Presentation & Communication | 10% | Is the demo clear and well-explained? |

## 7. Rules & Protocols
*   All projects must be developed during the event window (1 Apr – 18 Apr 2026).
*   Pre-built, copied, or previously submitted work is strictly prohibited.
*   Participants must bring their own laptops and required accessories for the final demo.
*   Teams must register through the official portal before 10 April 2026.
*   Any programming language, API, or framework may be used.
*   Projects must focus on campus-centric or real-world problem solving.
*   Teams must maintain a public GitHub / code repository throughout the event.
*   Respectful conduct toward peers, mentors, and judges is mandatory at all times.
*   Decision of the judging panel is final and binding.
